Privacy Policy

App: Bhajan HUB (Lovism Bhajan HUB)  |  Effective date: 20 November 2026

Bhajan HUB is an app for reading devotional bhajan lyrics and God Angel Bani announcements. It is developed by Rabten and Gyanan Dawa Gurung ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

  • Anonymous install ID. The first time you open the app, it creates a random ID and sends it to our server so we can count how many devices use the app. It contains no name, phone number or other personal details.
  • Ratings and feedback you choose to send. If you rate the app or send feedback, we receive the star rating, your message, and the name you choose to type (optional).
  • Technical information. Like most online services, our server and hosting provider may record technical details of requests, such as IP address and request time, for security and to prevent abuse.
  • Administrator accounts. Only the app's administrators sign in (name and a private number). Regular users do not create accounts or sign in.

Stored on your phone: the install ID and the time you last read God Angel Bani messages (used to show the unread count). Uninstalling the app removes them.

We do not collect: your name or phone number (unless you type your name in feedback), location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or payment information.

2. How we use information

  • To count installs and show community statistics in the app.
  • To read your ratings and feedback and improve the app.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your information, and the app contains no advertising or analytics tools. We use these service providers:

  • Railway – cloud hosting for our server and database. Its servers may be located outside Bhutan.
  • Google Fonts – the app downloads fonts from Google, so Google receives your IP address and basic device information when fonts are fetched.

We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Retention and deletion

The install ID is kept while the app is in use. Feedback is kept until we delete it. Because the install ID is anonymous, we cannot tell which device it belongs to unless you give it to us. To ask us to delete feedback you sent, email us at the address below with the message you sent and the date.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and administrator functions are protected by a login. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.
